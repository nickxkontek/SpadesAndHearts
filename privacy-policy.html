Spades and Hearts — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Spades and Hearts is a card game you play with friends on the same local network (WiFi). This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, store, sell, or send any personal information to the developer or to any third party. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

What stays on your device

The app remembers the display name you type and a random ID so you can rejoin a game after a disconnect. This is stored only on your own device and can be removed by uninstalling the app.

Local network use

When you host or join a table, the app talks directly to other computers on your local network. Your display name, your moves in the game, and any chat messages you type are shared only with the other players at your table. Nothing is sent over the internet or to any server run by the developer. Chat messages are not saved after the table closes.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
